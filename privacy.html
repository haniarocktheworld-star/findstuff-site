隐私政策

找东西 / FindStuff · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日

技术支持首页

一句话总结：这个 App 不收集你的任何数据。
你的物品记录、照片和存放位置全部保存在你自己的设备上，不会上传到任何服务器。

1. 我们收集哪些数据

没有。本 App 不收集、不传输、不存储任何个人信息。我们没有账号系统，不需要你注册或登录，也不需要你的邮箱、手机号或姓名。

2. 你的数据保存在哪里

全部保存在你自己的设备上：

  • 物品、存放位置、备注等信息保存在 App 的本地数据库中；
  • 你拍摄或选择的照片保存在 App 的本地文件里；
  • 这些数据不会离开你的设备，除非你自己主动导出备份文件。

3. 网络与第三方

本 App 不包含任何统计、分析、广告或追踪组件，也不与任何第三方共享数据。我们无法查看、也无法获取你的任何记录。

App 只会在你使用「购买完整版」或「恢复购买」时与 Apple 的 App Store 通信，该流程完全由 Apple 处理。

4. 权限说明

  • 相机：只有在你主动点击「拍照」时才会使用。照片仅保存在本机，不会上传。
  • 相册：通过系统提供的选择器读取你主动选中的那一张照片，App 无法浏览或访问你的相册。

如果你不使用拍照功能，可以随时在「设置 → 找东西」中关闭相机权限，App 其余功能不受影响。

5. 应用内购买

完整版通过 Apple 的应用内购买出售。支付流程完全由 Apple 处理，我们不接触、也无法获取你的付款信息。

6. 备份文件

你可以导出备份文件。该文件由你自己保管（例如存入「文件」App、iCloud 云盘或本机），App 不会上传它。请妥善保管，因为文件中包含你记录的物品信息。

7. 儿童隐私

本 App 不收集任何人的数据，因此也不会收集儿童的数据。

8. 删除你的数据

删除 App 即可删除全部数据。删除后无法恢复，建议提前导出备份。

9. 政策变更

如果本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页并修改生效日期。

10. 联系我们

如有任何疑问，请发送邮件至 rayjzheng@icloud.com。

Privacy Policy

FindStuff · Effective date: October 4, 2026

In one sentence: this app collects none of your data.
Your item records, photos, and storage locations stay entirely on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

1. What data we collect

None. This app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. There is no account system — you never register, sign in, or provide an email address, phone number, or name.

2. Where your data lives

Entirely on your own device:

  • Items, storage locations, and notes are kept in the app's local database;
  • Photos you take or choose are kept in the app's local files;
  • This data does not leave your device unless you export a backup file yourself.

3. Network and third parties

This app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking components, and shares no data with any third party. We have no way to see or obtain your records.

The app communicates with Apple's App Store only when you use "Unlock Full Version" or "Restore Purchases". That process is handled entirely by Apple.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you tap the camera button. Photos stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Photo library: the app reads only the single photo you pick through the system picker. It cannot browse or access your library.

If you don't use the camera feature, you can turn off camera access at any time in Settings → FindStuff; the rest of the app keeps working.

5. In-app purchase

The full version is sold through Apple's in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive or have access to your payment details.

6. Backup files

You can export a backup file. That file is yours to keep (for example in the Files app, iCloud Drive, or on-device); the app never uploads it. Please store it safely, as it contains the item information you recorded.

7. Children's privacy

This app collects no data from anyone, and therefore none from children either.

8. Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of your data. This cannot be undone, so please export a backup first.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date.

10. Contact

Questions? Email rayjzheng@icloud.com.
